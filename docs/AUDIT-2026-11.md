### The watercolour paintings (D1)

All landing art lives in `src/components/landing/`:

- `paint.ts`: pure, seeded drawing helpers. Mountain ridgelines by midpoint
  displacement, shadowed east faces, snow caps with fingers down the gullies,
  foliage clumps, conifers, pencil grass, ribbons for roads and rivers.
  Deterministic, so server and client draw identical shapes.
- `watercolour.tsx`: the paint filters (`wash`: wandering wet edges, mottled
  pigment, a darker dried rim; `soft`: skies and glows; `pencil`: underdrawing;
  `grain`: the paper) and the `Painting` sheet with a deckled paper edge.
- `scenes.tsx`: the five scenes. The long road (snow-capped ranges, foothill
  pines, mist, a plain, a river and a road to the horizon), the hearth hills at
  dusk, the sea-gate (headland temple, lighthouse on the rocks, grey sea), an
  inked map in the old cartographers' style, and painted cloth banners.
- Colours are tokens (`--wc-*` in `src/styles/design-tokens.css`): day is a pale
  morning on warm paper, night is the same painting as a moonlit nocturne.

Performance rules the art keeps: filtered paint never moves inside its own SVG
(filters are costly to redraw); only unfiltered overlays drift (mist, smoke,
birds, the beam), only while on screen, and nothing moves under reduced motion.
The hero's parallax moves whole layers as composited boxes, not SVG groups.
