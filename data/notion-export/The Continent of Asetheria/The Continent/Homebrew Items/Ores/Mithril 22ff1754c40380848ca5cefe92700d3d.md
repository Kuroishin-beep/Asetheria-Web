# Mithril

Description: A silvery-white metal that gleams with an inner light, as if moonbeams were trapped within its crystalline structure. Mithril appears almost translucent in thin sheets, yet maintains incredible strength and durability.
Location: Found exclusively in the deepest mountain caverns where underground rivers have flowed for millennia. The ore forms in veins that follow ancient ley lines, appearing as shimmering threads in the rock face.
Lore: Legend speaks of the first mithril being tears of the moon goddess, shed for mortal heroes who fought valiantly in her name. Ancient texts claim that armor made of pure mithril can turn aside even death itself, though such pieces are beyond rare.
Properties: Lighter than silk yet stronger than steel
Naturally resistant to corrosion and tarnish
Conducts magical energy with minimal loss
Self-repairing when exposed to moonlight
Uses: Favored by elven smiths for crafting armor that moves like cloth, weapons that never dull, and jewelry that enhances spellcasting. Mages weave thin mithril wire into their robes to improve spell focus.
