# Nullstone

Description: A completely matte gray stone that seems to exist in a state of absolute stillness. No light reflects from its surface, no sound echoes off it, and even the air around it appears motionless.
Location: Found in areas where magic has been catastrophically drained or destroyed. These dead zones appear as perfectly circular clearings where nothing grows and no magic functions, with nullstone forming at their centers like scabs over reality's wounds.
Lore: Nullstone is absence given form - not merely resistant to magic, but its complete antithesis. Mages speak of it in hushed tones, as prolonged exposure can permanently drain one's magical abilities, leaving them forever cut off from the arcane arts.
Properties: Completely immune to all magical effects
Creates dead magic zones in its vicinity
Suppresses magical abilities in living beings
Unaffected by any form of energy
Uses: Carved into cells and restraints for captured spellcasters, ground into powder to create anti-magic barriers, and fashioned into armor by those who hunt mages. Some desperate rulers line their throne rooms with nullstone to prevent magical assassination.
