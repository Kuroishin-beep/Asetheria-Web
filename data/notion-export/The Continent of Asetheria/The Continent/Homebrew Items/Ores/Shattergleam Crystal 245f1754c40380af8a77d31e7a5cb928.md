# Shattergleam Crystal

Description: Appears like a natural geode split open—filled with needle-thin, translucent spikes of shimmering crystal. These crystals radiate a faint, cold-blue glow, making them easily mistaken for magical or alchemical materials.
The outer structure is silvery-gray and flaked, like dried clay, crumbling if disturbed.
Location: Found underground in isolated caves undistrubed for years, or in magic rich areas
Lore: • Dwarves call it “Mhalaz-dur” or “Earth’s Cruel Joke,” and leave it untouched as a superstition—bad luck befalls miners who try to collect it.
• The drow sometimes carve cave warnings near it, believing it to be a “false star” laid by trickster gods to lure the greedy.
• Some sages speculate that Shattergleam is the fossilized light of an ancient divine event, frozen in time—but the truth remains unknown.
Properties: • Brittle Beyond Use:
    ◦ Shatters into dangerous splinters at heavy physical touch.
   
• Illusory Potential (non-magical):
    ◦ Reflects light strangely, sometimes creating ghostly shapes or halos in darkness, which some explorers misinterpret as magical or spiritual signs.
    ◦ Attempts to analyze or use it in magic, smithing, or alchemy have all failed—the dust evaporates within hours of exposure to air.
