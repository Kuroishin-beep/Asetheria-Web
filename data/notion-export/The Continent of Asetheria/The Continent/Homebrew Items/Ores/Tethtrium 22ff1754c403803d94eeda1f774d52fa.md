# Tethtrium

Description: A peculiar metal that exists in a state of constant, subtle motion. Its surface ripples like liquid mercury while maintaining solid form, and it seems to bend light around itself, creating rainbow halos at its edges.
Location: Forms in areas where the barriers between dimensions have worn thin, typically near ancient portals or sites where planar magic has been extensively practiced. It appears to seep through reality itself, crystallizing into metal as it enters the material plane.
Lore: Named after the first planar scholar to successfully study it, tethtrium is believed to be matter from the space between worlds. Working with it requires great skill, as inexperienced smiths have been known to phase partially out of existence, becoming trapped between dimensions.
Properties: Phases in and out of reality unpredictably
Allows limited passage through solid matter
Disrupts dimensional barriers
Unpredictable in its behavior
Uses: Crafted into tools for planar researchers, locks that exist in multiple dimensions simultaneously, and weapons that can strike incorporeal foes. Thieves prize tethtrium lockpicks that can reach through solid doors.
