# Embersteel

Description: A warm, orange-red metal that glows with the steady light of a forge's heart. Tiny sparks occasionally dance across its surface, and it radiates comfortable warmth without ever becoming too hot to handle safely.
Location:  Forms in the aftermath of great fires - not mere flames, but conflagrations of legendary proportions that burn with passion and purpose. Dragon lairs, sites of phoenix rebirth, and battlefields where fire elementals have fallen all eventually yield embersteel deposits.
Lore: Embersteel is said to hold the memory of every fire that contributed to its creation. Weapons made from it carry the courage of heroes and the fury of dragons, inspiring their wielders to acts of legendary bravery. Some believe that embersteel items grow stronger with each heroic deed performed with them
Properties: Maintains constant warmth without external heat
Becomes more powerful when exposed to flame
Gradually repairs itself over time
Inspires courage and determination in its wielder
Uses: Forged into weapons that never cool, armor that protects against cold, and hearths that burn without fuel. Warriors value embersteel blades for their ability to cauterize wounds and their morale-boosting properties in battle.
