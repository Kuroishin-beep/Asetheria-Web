# Cold Iron

Description:  A dark, almost black metal with a matte finish that seems to absorb light rather than reflect it. Its surface is always cool to the touch, regardless of ambient temperature, and frost often forms on its edges in humid conditions.
Location: Mined from regions where the boundary between the material world and otherworldly realms grows thin. These deposits are typically found in forgotten battlefields, ancient burial grounds, and places where powerful magic has been violently disrupted.
Lore: Born from the primordial conflict between order and chaos, cold iron represents the world's natural resistance to otherworldly influence. Blacksmiths who work with it must wear thick gloves, as prolonged contact can numb the soul itself
Properties: Naturally anathema to fey and fiendish creatures
Disrupts magical effects on contact
Remains perpetually cold, never warming
Causes painful burns to creatures of pure magic
Uses: Forged into weapons and restraints specifically designed to combat supernatural threats. Templars and witch hunters prize cold iron blades, while scholars use cold iron implements to safely handle cursed artifacts.
