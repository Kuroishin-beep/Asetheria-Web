# Adamantium

Description: The hardest known substance, appearing as a deep blue-black metal with an almost mirror-like polish. Its surface shows intricate natural patterns resembling flowing water frozen in time.
Location: Forms only in the hearts of volcanoes where elemental fire and earth energies reach perfect equilibrium. These rare nodes exist deep within active volcanic chambers, making extraction extraordinarily dangerous.
Lore: Said to be fragments of the world's original foundation, adamantium represents absolute permanence. Ancient kings would commission single adamantium items as symbols of their eternal dynasties, though ironically, most of these kingdoms have long since fallen to dust.
Properties: Naturally anathema to fey and fiendish creatures
Disrupts magical effects on contact
Remains perpetually cold, never warming
Causes painful burns to creatures of pure magic
Uses: Reserved for the most critical applications - vault doors, prison bars for the most dangerous criminals, and weapons meant to last forever. Master enchanters use adamantium focuses that never lose their potency.
