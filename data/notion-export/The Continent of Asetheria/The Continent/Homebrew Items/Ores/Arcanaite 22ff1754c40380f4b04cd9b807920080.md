# Arcanaite

Description: A crystalline material that shifts through the spectrum of colors, never settling on any single hue for long. It pulses with inner radiance and seems to hum with barely contained magical energy.
Location: Crystallizes naturally in areas of intense magical activity - wizard towers, sites of great battles between spellcasters, and nexuses where multiple ley lines converge. It grows like living crystal, forming geometric patterns of breathtaking beauty.
Lore: Scholars debate whether arcanaite is magic given physical form or matter transformed by pure magical energy. What is certain is that large deposits can become self-aware, leading to the terrifying phenomenon known as "living dungeons.”
Properties: Stores and amplifies magical energy
Changes properties based on the type of magic it absorbs
Resonates with nearby spellcasting
Unstable in large quantities
Uses: Ground into powder for the most potent spell components, carved into focuses and spell gems, and used as currency among the most powerful mages. Artificers embed small arcanaite crystals in magical items to boost their effectiveness.
