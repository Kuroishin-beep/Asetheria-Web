# Nullcap (Neutral Mushroom)

Description:  A small, grayish-white mushroom with a translucent cap that glows faintly in total darkness. Found in clusters along damp cave walls and abandoned mines.
Effects: • Neutral — no known magical or alchemical properties.
• Creatures sometimes use them as markers in dark environments due to their faint bioluminescence.
Lore: Some say the Nullcap is the "ghost" of once-powerful fungi, drained of purpose but still clinging to the Underdark.
Scientific Name: Mycena obscura
