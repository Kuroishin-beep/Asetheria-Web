# Embergill Bloom (Beneficial Mushroom)

Description: A golden-orange mushroom with frilled gills that radiate soft warmth. Found in geothermal caves near lava vents or hot springs.
Effects: • When brewed into a tea or eaten raw, it grants 1d4 temporary hit points and advantage on saving throws against disease for 24 hours.
• Has mild healing properties when turned into a salve.
Lore: Revered by deep gnomes (Svirfneblin) as a gift from the earth's heart. Often used in healing rituals or given as offerings to earth spirits.
Scientific Name: Luminomyces vitalis
