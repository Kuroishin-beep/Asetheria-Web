# Widow’s Bell (Harmful Mushroom)

Description: A dark purple, bell-shaped mushroom that releases faint melodic chimes when disturbed. The sound is eerie and hypnotic.
Effects: • Inhalation of spores causes a mild hallucinogenic effect followed by paralysis within 1 minute (DC 14 CON save or paralyzed for 1d4 hours).
• Handling it unprotected exposes one to the spores.
Lore: Believed to grow where widows have wept or where mournful songs have echoed in the deep. Drow sometimes use it in poisons.
Scientific Name: Bellamyces mortifera
