# Notable

Created: October 13, 2024 11:04 AM

### **Geography & Location**

- **Ekran**:
    - A harsh, unforgiving land where Mycairos established itself after arriving as exiles.
    - Characterized by rugged cliffs and sprawling hills, ideal for the construction of fortress cities.
    - The landscape includes fertile valleys, but the harsh conditions require strong defenses.

### **Major Cities & Citadels**

- **Citadel of Mycarion**:
    - The heart of the Mycairos empire, serving as the political and spiritual center.
    - A massive fortress carved into the cliffs, designed to withstand siege and storm.
    - Home to kings, priests, and sorcerers who conducted mysterious rituals in secret chambers.
- **Fortress Cities**:
    - Scattered across the empire, each ruled by a warrior-king with a loyal and fearsome army.
    - Designed for defense and intimidation, these cities emphasize military might over civilian infrastructure.
    - Architecture combines natural stone with brutalist designs, conveying power and resilience.

---

### **Cultural Aspects**

- **Linear B Runes**:
    - The written language of Mycairos, used for recording history, rituals, and laws.
    - Many texts remain undeciphered, particularly those containing warnings or insights about hidden truths.
- **Mythology**:
    - Mycairos claims descent from gods of war and fire, which is woven into their cultural identity.
    - Fragments of myth suggest a history of exile and pacts with forgotten beings, hinting at darker origins.
- **Brutal Magic**:
    - Mycairos favored aggressive magic—wards of blood and stone, curses, and indestructible weapons.
    - Their sorcerers engaged in dark rituals, hinting at a culture steeped in power at the cost of morality.

### **Artifacts & Relics**

- **Tablets of Mycarion**:
    - Inscribed with Linear B runes, these tablets document historical events, rituals, and the phrase “the gift beneath stone.”
    - Scholars debate the true meaning, speculating on hidden treasures or awakened powers.
- **Cursed Weapons**:
    - Forged with blood magic, these weapons are said to carry the souls of those who wielded them, gaining power from their victims.
    - Legends claim that some weapons can bring ruin to their enemies but turn against their bearers if used without respect.

---

### **Mysterious Events**

- **Strange Disappearances**:
    - Accounts of nobility vanishing without a trace, leading to increasing paranoia within the empire.
    - Possible connections to the ancient pacts and rituals, suggesting darker forces at play.
- **Final Days of Mycairos**:
    - Reports of storms carrying voices and shadows moving against the light, creating an atmosphere of dread.
    - Madness began to spread, causing distrust among the ranks and leading to the construction of more fortifications.
- **Unearthed Forces**:
    - Evidence suggests that Mycairos unearthed an ancient, slumbering force that contributed to their downfall.
    - Carvings found in abandoned ruins depict creatures and symbols never before seen, hinting at a reality beyond their understanding.

---

### **Decline & Fall**

- **Sudden Collapse**:
    - The empire's fall came abruptly, with cities left abandoned and citadels echoing with silence.
    - Survivors reported a pervasive sense of being watched and an unease that drove them from the ruins.
- **Legacy of Ruins**:
    - The once-mighty citadels now stand as reminders of a civilization consumed by its own ambition and fear.
    - Entering these ruins is considered dangerous, as they are said to be haunted by the remnants of the past.
