# Triggers

Created: October 13, 2024 11:41 AM

### **Quest Triggers**

1. **Ancient Prophecy**
    - The characters discover a fragment of a prophecy that mentions the "Third Sun" and hints at an impending disaster linked to both empires. As omens begin to manifest—strange storms, strange disappearances—the party is tasked with uncovering the truth behind the prophecy and its connection to the ruins.
2. **Disturbance in the Ruins**
    - Recent events in the ruins of **Mycairos**—such as strange shadows, echoes of voices, and sightings of unearthly creatures—draw the attention of local authorities. The party is hired to investigate the disturbance, leading them into the heart of the once-great empire where they uncover deeper mysteries.
3. **Artifact Awakening**
    - One of the party members discovers an ancient artifact (a ring, amulet, or weapon) during a mundane task. Upon touching it, they experience vivid visions of the lost empires and are compelled to seek out its origin. The artifact may be tied to the **gift beneath stone**, pointing them toward the **Citadel of Mycarion** or the **Palace of Eronis**.
4. **The Scholar's Curse**
    - A well-respected scholar or magic-user becomes afflicted by a curse tied to the ruins. Their condition worsens, leading to visions of the past and madness. The party must delve into the ruins to find a way to lift the curse, leading them to discover the dark history of both empires.
5. **The Relic of Power**
    - A powerful relic from Mycairos, capable of summoning ancient warriors or dark forces, is said to have resurfaced. Competing factions want to claim it for themselves, and the characters must race against time to locate and secure the relic before it falls into the wrong hands.
6. **Haunting Whisper**
    - An unsettling phenomenon begins to affect a nearby settlement, with townsfolk reporting whispers in the night and visions of shadowy figures. Local leaders ask the adventurers to investigate the source of these disturbances, leading them to the ruins of **Mycairos** or **Nethrana**, where they must confront the echoes of the past.
