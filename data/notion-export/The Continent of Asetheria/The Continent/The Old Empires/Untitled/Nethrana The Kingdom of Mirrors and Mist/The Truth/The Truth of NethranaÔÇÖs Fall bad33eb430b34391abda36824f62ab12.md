# The Truth of Nethrana’s Fall

Created: October 13, 2024 11:02 AM

Nethrana’s downfall began not with armies or curses, but with **hubris**—the slow corruption born from a mastery over illusion and mind. At the height of their power, Nethranan dream-walkers discovered a dangerous truth: **reality itself was malleable**. Their sorcerers found ways to shift perception on a massive scale, crafting not just individual illusions but **entire layers of reality**, twisting the waking world and dreams into one.

The Council of Eronis, hungry for more, sought to **pierce the boundaries between worlds**. Using their portals and enchanted mirrors, they reached into **the Echo Plane**—a shadow reflection of existence where time bends and memories linger like ghosts. But what they found there was **not empty**. In the Echo Plane lurked **fractured gods**—beings without form, cast off from creation, desperate to return to the material world.

Through hubris or ignorance, the Council struck a **bargain** with these entities, promising them a place within Nethrana’s dreams. But the gods of the Echo Plane were deceitful. They fed off the minds of the dream-walkers, slowly unraveling the boundaries between illusion and reality until the Nethranans could no longer tell the difference. Their most skilled sorcerers **vanished into their own reflections**, trapped in endless labyrinths of thought.

As their minds fractured, so did their empire. Cities became haunted by illusions—phantoms of the past, fragments of the dreamscape leaking into the physical world. Entire regions vanished overnight, **not destroyed, but unmade**, as if they had never existed. Nethrana’s magic turned on itself, consuming the very foundations of their power. Some say that the **last Council session** ended with its members staring into mirrors that reflected not their own faces, but the faces of the gods they had bargained with.

When the Mycairos armies finally arrived at Velothis, they found **no resistance**, only empty streets and crumbling halls—an empire that had already collapsed inward, lost to its own illusions. What became of the Nethranans is unknown, though explorers whisper that some still wander the ruins, **not as ghosts but as living dreamers**, forever trapped in visions they cannot escape.
