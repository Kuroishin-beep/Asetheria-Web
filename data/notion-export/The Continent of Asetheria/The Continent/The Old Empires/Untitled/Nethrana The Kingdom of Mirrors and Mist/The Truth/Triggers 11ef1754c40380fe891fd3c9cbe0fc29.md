## **Quest Triggers for the Lost Empire of Nethrana**

### 1. **Ancient Prophecy Unearthed**

- **Trigger**: The adventurers stumble upon a forgotten tome or scroll in an old library or ruins. This tome contains fragmented prophecies about the **Third Sun** and the fate of Nethrana, hinting at treasures and knowledge buried within the ruins.
- **Call to Action**: The prophecy mentions a celestial alignment approaching soon, suggesting that the time to uncover Nethrana's secrets is now or face dire consequences.

### 2. **A Mysterious Artifact**

- **Trigger**: One of the characters discovers an ancient artifact—perhaps the **Dreamer’s Lens** or **Eronis Gate-Stone**—while exploring a different ruin or market. The artifact emanates a strange energy and is tied to Nethrana.
- **Call to Action**: As they examine it, visions or echoes of the past begin to unfold, revealing glimpses of Nethrana and its former glory. An enigmatic figure (possibly a spectral dreamer) appears, urging them to find the **Palace of Eronis** before it’s too late.

### 3. **Strange Dreams and Nightmares**

- **Trigger**: One or more characters begin experiencing vivid dreams or nightmares about Nethrana. They may encounter lost souls or visions of the palace and its wonders—each dream ends with a cryptic warning.
- **Call to Action**: Upon waking, they find clues left in their dreams (such as glyphs or images) that guide them to seek out the lost empire and discover the truth behind the **curse of the dreamers**.

### 4. **Encounters with Wanderers**

- **Trigger**: The party encounters a group of **Wanderers**—individuals who have been cursed or lost in dreams. These Wanderers speak of their memories of Nethrana and plead for help to break the curse and restore their homeland.
- **Call to Action**: The adventurers must delve into the ruins to find artifacts or knowledge that can free the Wanderers and uncover the truth behind their transformation.

### 5. **The Call of a Forgotten God**

- **Trigger**: A priest or oracle in a nearby settlement claims to have received a vision or message from a forgotten deity associated with Nethrana. The god beseeches the adventurers to reclaim its lost artifacts and restore balance to the world.
- **Call to Action**: The adventurers are given a divine quest to explore the ruins, seeking both the artifacts and the truth behind the god's disappearance and its connection to the **Great War with Mycairos**.
