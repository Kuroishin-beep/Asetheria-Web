## **Notable Features of Nethrana**

### **Geography & Location**

- **Velothis (Island of Nethrana)**:
    - An enigmatic island believed to have risen from the sea or been reshaped by ancient magic.
    - Surrounded by turbulent waters and hidden currents, accessible only through enchanted trade routes.
    - Lush coastlines with strange flora that shimmer under moonlight and fade at dawn.
    - Dense, mist-filled forests inland, rumored to hide remnants of portals or dormant magic.

### **Major Cities & Ruins**

- **Eronis (Capital City)**:
    - Location of the **Palace of Eronis**, a labyrinth of enchanted glass and stone.
    - Famous for endless shifting corridors that confound explorers, with **portals** rumored to lead into dreamscapes.
    - The **Council of Eronis** is believed to have ruled here—dream-walkers and illusionists whose influence spanned the empire and beyond.
- **Irelas (Port City of Trade)**:
    - Known for its magnificent docks and enchanted vessels, which transported illusions, textiles, and magical artifacts.
    - Many shipwrecks from Irelas have been found across distant seas, each carrying strange cargo that defies understanding.
    - The city now lies in ruin, with its crumbled piers giving way to coral formations that glow with residual magic.
- **Lytheris (Fortress-City)**:
    - A military stronghold near the southern cliffs of Velothis, facing the kingdom of Mycairos across the sea.
    - Rumored to have housed a **forbidden artifact** tied to the downfall of Nethrana—perhaps a weapon that backfired on its creators.
    - Now abandoned, the city is shrouded in perpetual fog, with spectral lights seen flickering in the night.

---

### **Artifacts of Nethrana**

- **The Dreamer’s Lens**:
    - An enchanted glass orb that, when gazed into, allows the user to see fragmented visions of alternate futures and pasts.
    - Prolonged use causes the lines between dreams and reality to blur for the viewer, risking madness.
- **The Soulweave Tapestry**:
    - A tapestry woven with threads of memory and illusion. When touched, it replays events from the past, but only from the perspective of someone who lived them.
    - Some believe the tapestry holds the memories of Nethrana’s final days—but only fragments remain.
- **Eronis Gate-Stones**:
    - Smooth black stones engraved with shifting glyphs, used to activate portals throughout the empire.
    - These artifacts still pulse with weak magic and are sought after by scholars trying to unlock the **dream-gates** hidden within the Palace of Eronis.
- **The Mask of Reveries**:
    - A ceremonial mask worn by seers of the Council of Eronis. It grants the wearer the ability to glimpse the thoughts of others but distorts their own sense of identity with prolonged use.
    - Some say the mask itself has a mind of its own, whispering secrets to those who dare wear it.

---

### **Notable Features of Culture and Magic**

- **Pictorial Language of Nethrana**:
    - Symbols and glyphs that shift meaning depending on light and angle. Scholars find it nearly impossible to translate, as the glyphs seem to reflect the viewer’s own thoughts.
- **Illusion Magic as a Cultural Staple**:
    - The Nethranans considered illusion to be an art form, blending magic with architecture and trade.
    - Murals in ruins seem to come alive, displaying different scenes depending on the time of day.
- **Dream-Walking Rituals**:
    - The Council of Eronis practiced **dream-walking**, entering dreams to gather knowledge or influence the waking world.
    - It is said that Nethranan sorcerers could trap enemies within endless dreams, making them prisoners of their own minds.

---

### **Mysterious Disappearance and Fall**

- **The Prophecy of the Third Sun**:
    - “Beware the Third Sun, for what is seen shall not be saved.” The meaning of this inscription remains unclear, but some speculate it foretold the collapse of Nethrana or the arrival of a cosmic event.
- **Curse of the Dreamers**:
    - Legends claim the Nethranan people succumbed to a subtle curse, wandering eternally within dreams. This curse may have turned their magic against them, making it impossible to distinguish illusion from reality.
- **The Great War with Mycairos**:
    - Nethrana’s fall is often linked to a war with the distant kingdom of Mycairos, though the true nature of their conflict is lost to time.
    - Some believe Nethrana was betrayed from within, while others claim it was undone by an uncontrollable magic born from its own people.
