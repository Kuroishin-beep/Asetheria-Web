# The Rifts

## History:

The rifts first appeared after the primordial meteor struck during the continental war. This fanned the flames of war even further, with a newfound source of weapons and armor—ores and unrecognizable jewels pulsing with power.

This sparked much more intense battles, with all sides fighting their hardest. Magical properties collided, creatures were summoned to aid in the war, and portals from different planes of existence were called upon. Because of this, rifts began opening at random places and times. When the rifts appeared, the connection to the different planes of existence was severed by a gray fog that none could pass through, rebuffing any who tried.

Different things emerged from the rifts: creatures both intelligent and inept, as well as equipment, potions, herbs, and scrolls or books containing varied contents, from random diaries and poetry to advanced books of technology.

The rifts became a permanent fixture in the world, appearing and disappearing at random intervals. Scholars and mages dedicated their lives to studying these phenomena, attempting to predict their occurrences and understand their nature. Despite years of research, the true origin and purpose of the rifts remained a mystery, fueling countless theories and legends.

## Present:

Even after the fires of war were quenched, the rifts continued to appear, albeit with less frequency. Their erratic nature remained a wellspring of both wonder and dread among the peoples of the world. Scholars, seekers, and adventurers pursued the mysteries of these dimensional tears, driven by dreams of wielding their power or, perhaps, averting the catastrophes they might portend.

The realms beyond each rift shift with every appearance, unfixed in form or familiarity. Yet, strange patterns sometimes emerge, as though these portals hint at an underlying design. In one notable instance, two rifts on opposite sides of the continent led to the same plane of existence, suggesting an unseen linkage between these dimensional tears.

A tale still spoken of in the halls of scholarship recounts the opening of a Bound Rift, wherein the past of the continent lay preserved within. Beyond its threshold, ruined empires stood whole once more, and arts thought lost to time were practiced daily by hands long turned to dust. For but an hour the rift yawned open, offering brief glimpses into the technologies and techniques of ancient days. Those brave enough to enter recorded what they could, but when the rift closed, none returned. Scholars have since scoured every clue, seeking signs of what those lost souls might have uncovered within—but no trace has yet come to light.

The Scholars have classified the types of portal into three: Bound, Shifting, and Anchored.

### Bound Rifts:

Bound Rifts are a type of rift stable enough to allow creatures to pass freely between worlds with no apparent repercussions. The lifespan of these rifts varies greatly, lasting anywhere from a single hour to a decade. Scholars are baffled by this inconsistency and have yet to find any discernable pattern or correlation among Bound Rifts that might explain these differences.

Seekers may enter these rifts to gather resources, interact with inhabitants, and even bring objects back to their own world. However, the inhabitants of these other realms are bound within their own worlds and cannot cross into ours, a fact that continues to mystify scholars.

These rifts are most often created through specific rituals, surges of magical energy, or powerful clashes that distort the fabric of reality.

### Shifting Rifts

Shifting Rifts are unpredictable, appearing suddenly without warning. Unlike Bound Rifts, no living creature from our world can enter a Shifting Rift; any attempt to do so results in dire consequences, with seekers barely escaping with their lives and bearing marks no scholar can explain.

While no one from our side can explore these realms directly, beings from within the rift—be they friend or foe—may emerge into our world, often accompanied by artifacts ranging from the common to the legendary. The technology to peer into these rifts is nonexistent, and scholars remain confounded by the mysterious barrier that prevents entry.

These rifts are known to form during intense magical battles, sudden surges of concentrated magical energy, reality-bending conflicts, or seemingly at random.

These rifts are made either via a strong battle with magic, a strong surge of concentratead magic, a battle that distorts reality, or randomly

### Anchored Rifts

Anchored Rifts are a special subset of Bound Rifts that have been tethered to our world by the use of an anchoring crystal. Through years of experimentation, scholars discovered a crystal capable of "anchoring" a rift to our plane. The process remains unstable, however, as attempts to tether Bound Rifts often fail due to unknown variables. The exact method of creating these crystals has since been lost, for the Bound Rift originally used for testing closed unexpectedly, severing trade routes and halting production of the anchoring crystals.

Like Bound Rifts, Anchored Rifts allow beings from our world to freely explore, gather resources, and interact within the rift, but the creatures within the rift are still unable to cross into our realm. No known side effects of Anchored Rifts have yet been observed, though scholars speculate that certain rifts may be impossible to anchor due to their immense size or the advanced technologies they contain.

Anchored Rifts are formed when a Bound Rift is tethered by a specially crafted anchoring crystal, binding it to our world for an extended period.
