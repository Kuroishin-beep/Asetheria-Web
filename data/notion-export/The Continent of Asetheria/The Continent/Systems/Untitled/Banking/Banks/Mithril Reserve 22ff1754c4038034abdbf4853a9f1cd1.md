# Mithril Reserve

Created: July 13, 2025 12:06 PM
Motto: Motto: Fides in Metallo, Fiducia in Lapide (Trust in Metal, Faith in Stone)

## Overview

The Mithril Reserve is not merely a place to store gold; it is a pillar of commerce and political power in Aetheria. Founded by the Merca Family, its principles are rooted in dwarven tradition: strength, integrity, and an unshakable memory for debts. The bank's headquarters is carved into a mountain at the heart of the continent, but it maintains formidable stone branches in every major city.

## Core Concepts

### **The *Argentar* (The Banker)**

The Mithril Reserve has a  *Argentar*. These are the licensed, public-facing members of the bank. They are not mere tellers; they are financial advisors, agents of the Reserve, and respected figures in their communities. To become an *Argentar* is to swear an oath upon a shard of the original Mithril Lode.

### The Stonework Ledger (The Records)

All official transactions in the Mithril Reserve are chiseled onto massive, basalt ledgers kept in the vaults of each branch. For major accounts or inter-branch transfers, a magical duplicate of the inscription is simultaneously carved onto a master ledger in the mountain headquarters via a network of linked Runestones. This creates a permanent, tamper-proof record.

### ***Fides* (Trust and Reputation)**

The Reserve's entire business model is built on its reputation for being unbreachable, incorruptible, and utterly reliable. They guard this reputation more fiercely than their gold. Slandering the Reserve or successfully defrauding it is met with overwhelming and relentless retribution, both legal and... extra-

## How to Bank with Mithril Reserve

### **Step 1: Opening an Account (The *Depositum*)**

This is a formal process, not for commoners with a few silver pieces. The Reserve deals with merchant guilds, noble houses, kingdoms, and successful adventuring parties.

1. **The Approach:** The party must enter a branch of the Mithril Reserve. These buildings are marvels of defensive architecture—solid stone, few windows, and guarded by elite, heavily-armored *Custodes*. They will be directed to the main hall, a grand, echoing chamber where several *Argentars* sit at massive stone desks.
2. **The Verification:** The party must prove their "standing." This can be done with a letter of introduction from a noble, a guild charter, or, most commonly for adventurers, a **"Founding Deposit"** of at least 1,000 gold pieces. This proves they are serious clients.
3. **The Mark of Attunement:** This is the fantasy equivalent of a signature and PIN. The *Argentar* produces a small, smooth obsidian stone set in a lead tray. The primary account holder must place a single drop of their blood onto the stone. The *Argentar* then performs a small ritual, tapping the stone with a silver hammer and chanting in Dwarvish. The stone absorbs the blood and will henceforth glow with a faint inner light only when held by the person whose blood it took. This is their **Attunement Mark**. It is unique and nearly impossible to forge.
4. **The Deposit & The *Tessera*:** Once the funds are counted and verified (often by a gnome *Tabularius*, or clerk, known for their sharp eyes), the transaction is chiseled into the Stonework Ledger. The party is then given a **Tessera**—a small, heavy coin made of lead or bronze, stamped with their unique account number and the seal of the Mithril Reserve. This, combined with their Attunement Mark, is the key to their fortune.

### **Step 2: Making a Withdrawal or Inquiry (The *Receptum*)**

1. **Presentation:** The account holder approaches an *Argentar* and presents their *Tessera*.
2. **Authentication:** The *Argentar* consults the ledger to find the account. They then present the account's Attunement Stone to the client. The client must grasp the stone. If it glows, their identity is confirmed. No glow, no transaction.
3. **Transaction:** The client states their business. The *Argentar* confirms the available funds and dispenses the requested amount. The new balance is immediately carved into the ledger, with the old entry struck through but left visible for auditing purposes.

### **Step 3: Letters of Credit (The *Permutatio Aethensis*)**

This is the most useful service for travelers and adventurers. It allows one to deposit money in one city and withdraw it in another, avoiding the immense risk of carrying thousands of coins on the road.

1. **The Request:** The client goes to their branch and requests a *Permutatio*. They specify the amount and the destination city.
2. **The Fee:** The Reserve charges a handsome fee for this service, typically 8-10% of the total amount. This is a major source of their income.
3. **The Tablet of Transfer:** The funds are withdrawn from their account. The *Argentar* then prepares a special, sealed clay tablet. Inside this hard-baked clay shell is a thin sheet of mithril, upon which the details of the transaction, the destination branch, and a complex arcane seal are inscribed. The clay shell is then sealed with the branch's unique wax seal, which is itself dusted with iron filings that are magically aligned to the origin branch.
4. **The Redemption:** The party travels to the destination city and presents the sealed tablet. The *Argentar* there will first examine the outer seal. They will use a special lodestone to check the alignment of the iron filings—a simple but effective first check against forgery. If it passes, they will carefully break the clay shell and examine the mithril plate and its arcane seal. For large sums, they will cast a *Sending* spell to the origin branch for immediate confirmation. Once verified, the funds are dispensed.

### **Step 4: Securing a Loan (The *Mutuum*)**

The Reserve will lend money, but they are ruthless in their terms.

1. **Collateral (*Pignus*):** No one gets a loan without collateral. The Reserve demands something of greater value than the loan itself. This could be the deed to a castle, a valuable magic item, a stake in a mine, or even a Geas placed on the borrower to perform a service for the bank if they default.
2. **Interest:** Interest rates are high, often 20-25%. The terms are explained in excruciating detail and magically bound.
3. **Default:** If a loan is not paid back on time, the collateral is seized without exception or pity. This has led to many ruined nobles and more than one adventure hook.

## Security & Personnel

- ***Dominus Argentar* (Bank Master):** Each branch is run by a Master Banker, often a senior dwarf from Clan Ironhand. They are powerful, well-connected, and not to be trifled with.
- ***Custodes* (The Guards):** The bank's guards are not city watch. They are professional, full-time soldiers, equipped with the best armor and weapons money can buy, often including magical items. They live to defend the bank.
- ***Arcanist-Securitas* (Mage-Security):** Every branch employs at least one full-time wizard or sorcerer whose only job is magical security. They maintain the wards, conduct magical forensics on forgeries, and provide offensive spellcasting in the event of an attack.
- **The Vaults:** The vaults of the Mithril Reserve are legendary.
    - **Outer Vaults:** Thick adamantine doors, complex mechanical locks requiring multiple keys held by different officers.
    - **Inner Vaults:** Protected by powerful magical wards: *Forbiddance* spells to block teleportation, *Glyphs of Warding* on every surface, and resident guardians like Stone Golems or even a Shield Guardian bound to the *Dominus Argentar*.
    - **Rumors:** It's rumored the central vault in the mountain headquarters is guarded by a slumbering ancient dragon, bound into service to the Reserve generations ago.
