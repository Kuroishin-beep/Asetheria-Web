### **I. *Legio Rubrum Obsidianum* ("The Red Obsidian Legion")**

*Theme: Numbered Red Knights & Black Knights (Overgeared elite order)*

- **Nickname:** *The Emperor’s Hammer*
- **Role:** Elite shock troops and battlefield dominators
- **Structure:**
    - **Centuria Rubra** – Red Knights, each known by a numerical title (e.g., *Primus, Secundus, Tertius*…), clad in crimson and gold, wielding enchanted arms and heavy armor.
    - **Cohors Umbrae** – Black Knights, enforcers of discipline and destruction, clad in obsidian plate, often used as executioners or to break enemy morale.
- **Specialization:** Heavy cavalry charges, magical arms and armor, field-command enforcement
- **Motto:** *"Ferio, Et Regno" — I Strike, and I Rule*

---

### **II. *Legio Custodia Praetoriana* ("The Praetorian Custodians")**

*Theme: Military Police / Internal Order*

- **Nickname:** *The Iron Judges*
- **Role:** Policing, internal discipline, and security of the Imperium
- **Structure:**
    - **Tribuni Urbani** – Urban enforcement officers handling civic order
    - **Cohors Exactorum** – Field arbiters deployed in active warzones to investigate crimes, corruption, or desertion
    - **Speculatores Interni** – Inquisitor-style agents for rooting out treason, cults, or forbidden magic
- **Specialization:** Law enforcement, suppression, psychological control
- **Motto:** *"Per Legem, Invictus" — Through Law, Unconquered*

---

### **III. *Legio Bestiarum Dominus* ("Beastlords Legion")**

*Theme: Beast taming and mounted warfare (land and air)*

- **Nickname:** *The Crimson Mane*
- **Role:** Mounted warfare, battlefield control, and beast deployment
- **Structure:**
    - **Alae Ferocis** – Land-mounted units riding dire wolves, lion-steeds, or scaled tuskers
    - **Aether Dracones** – Sky-riders mounted on gryphons, wyverns, and trained pegasi
    - **Bestiarii Magni** – Master beast tamers, often leading tamed monsters into battle
- **Specialization:** Animal-based warfare, terrain control, cavalry raids
- **Motto:** *"Dominor Naturae" — I Master Nature*

---

### **IV. *Legio Umbrae Sicarii* ("Shadow Assassins Legion")**

*Theme: Scouts, spies, assassins*

- **Nickname:** *The Cloaked Talons*
- **Role:** Reconnaissance, sabotage, targeted eliminations
- **Structure:**
    - **Speculatores** – Long-range scouts and forward agents
    - **Sicarii Nocturni** – Night assassins who strike with precision
    - **Velites Umbrarum** – Lightly armored skirmishers with stealth and agility
- **Specialization:** Stealth warfare, information control, covert operations
- **Motto:** *"Vidi. Veni. Vani." — I Saw. I Came. You Vanished.*

---

### **V. *Legio Arcanum Bellatorum* ("Warriors of the Arcane Legion")**

*Theme: Balanced force with martial, ranged, and magical elements; tanky and versatile*

- **Nickname:** *The Triform Host*
- **Role:** Versatile frontlines, arcane warfare, magical defense
- **Structure:**
    - **Manipuli Martiales** – Frontline soldiers trained in weapon mastery and shield formations
    - **Sagittarii Incantatores** – Arcane archers blending spell and shot
    - **Custodes Arcani** – Battle-mages and wardens who shield the legion and counter magical threats
- **Specialization:** Mixed combat roles, magic resilience, strategic versatility
- **Motto:** *"Corpus, Telum, Animus" — Body, Weapon, Soul*
