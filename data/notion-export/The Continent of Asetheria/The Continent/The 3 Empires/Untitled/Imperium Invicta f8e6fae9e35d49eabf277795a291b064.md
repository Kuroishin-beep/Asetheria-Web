# Introduction

*As transcribed by Imperial Archivist Quintus Arminius, Fourth Scribe of the Aeterna Lexicon*

---

The **Imperium Invicta** stands as a symbol of power, order, and civilization—a vast empire built on discipline, determination, and conquest. Stretching across distant provinces to the grand capital of **Aeterna City**, the Imperium thrives through military might and superior governance. Its citizens see themselves as architects of civilization, spreading law and progress wherever they go. Their motto, *“Vincit qui se vincit”*—*He conquers who conquers himself*—reflects their belief that self-discipline is the key to greatness.

At its heart lies **Aeterna City**, where the legendary hero **Iovianus Sandarion** first united the empire against chaos. The city’s marble temples, triumphal arches, and aqueducts stand as monuments to Invictan engineering and glory. Here, the Emperor reigns, surrounded by the Senate, which now advises and administers the far-reaching provinces.

The **Legions of the Imperium** are more than warriors—they are builders, constructing roads, fortresses, and aqueducts that link the empire's corners to its core. Their discipline ensures that where the Legions march, order follows. Gladiatorial arenas and public circuses entertain the masses with bloody spectacles that reflect the empire’s ideals of strength and survival.

The Imperium endures not because it is invincible but because it adapts and rises stronger with every challenge. To be Invictan is not merely to live—it is to excel.

---

## History of the Empire

---

Amidst the chaos of the Continental War, a time when the world was ravaged by infernal creatures, demonic forces, and warring gods, one man rose to prominence—Iovianus Sandarion, he fought not just to survive but to carve a haven for humanity. His enemies were beyond mortal comprehension: demons, gods, devils, and monstrous beings from the abyss. With unparalleled skill and unwavering resolve, Iovianus united the fragmented human territories, forming the foundation of a new empire in the heart of the war-torn lands.

Iovianus Sandarion didn't stand alone. He allied with two other great empires, forming a triumvirate of power. These empires became bulwarks of civilization, standing as a beacon of hope for the humans who had been on the brink of annihilation. Though the war was long and brutal, the victory brought hope and stability, and Iovanius’ empire, built on the principles of discipline, military prowess, and survival, stood strong.

However, the empire Iovanius forged became more than just a safe haven. As the war continued to rage, it became a highly militaristic state, where the values of discipline, strategy, and strength in battle were paramount. Every citizen was expected to contribute to the war effort, either by serving on the front lines or supporting those who did. The military was the backbone of the empire, with every decision and action aimed at sustaining its war machine.

Even when the Continental War drew to a close, and a treaty was forged between the three great empires, Iovanius’ empire retained its military structure. The scars of war ran deep, and the empire never let its guard down. But with the end of the war came a mystery—Iovianus Sandarion, the legendary hero, was said to be in seclusion in the capital city, mending and recuperating his wounds.

---

## The Empire Today

---

A decade has passed since the signing of the peace treaty, and while the horrors of war have largely faded from memory, the empire remains wary. Although peace reigns, and the once-active battlefields lie dormant, the shadow of the war lingers. Monsters still roam in the wilderness, and the other empires, though allies, are watched with cautious eyes. The empire Iovianus Sandarion built remains steadfast, clinging to its militaristic roots under the rule of the current emperor.

The empire continues to focus heavily on land-based warfare, with intricate strategies and formidable defenses in place to ensure that any threat can be swiftly neutralized. War may no longer be the empire's daily reality, but the military culture is so ingrained that even in times of peace, its generals and tacticians are constantly preparing for the next battle. The empire's soldiers are trained not only in combat but in strategy, logistics, and fortification, making them formidable opponents on any battlefield.

However, with the absence of active warfare, the empire has had to adapt its economy. During the war, it thrived on military production, but now, it has shifted its focus to more peaceful industries, although the militaristic infrastructure remains. Much like ancient Rome, the empire has turned to agriculture, fishing, and trade as its economic pillars. Farmlands stretch across the empire's vast territories, feeding the population and generating surplus crops for trade with neighboring nations. Fishing has become a vital industry, especially along the coastal regions, and the empire’s bustling ports are filled with ships importing exotic spices and goods from distant lands.

The rise of trade has also given birth to a thriving merchant class. Shipbuilding has become a lucrative industry, with massive fleets constructed to transport goods and people across the seas. As a result, the empire's economy has grown significantly, branching out from its military-industrial roots into commerce and agriculture. But while trade and industry flourish, the people of the empire never forget their militaristic heritage. Even the merchant class maintains a sense of discipline and duty, knowing that their wealth and success are ultimately protected by the empire’s enduring military might.

In this new era of peace, the empire's leaders must navigate the delicate balance between maintaining the military machine that has defined the nation's identity and fostering the economic and cultural growth necessary to secure its future. With Iovianus Sandarion and the other legends gone, the current emperor faces the daunting task of preserving the empire's legacy while adapting to a world that is no longer consumed by war—at least, not yet.

## **Introduction**

The **Imperium Invicta**, eternal and unshaken, is the pinnacle of mortal achievement—a civilization forged through unmatched discipline, moral purity, and divine favor. Its dominion extends from the windswept highlands of the North to the sun-scorched dunes of the South, all governed with justice and reason from the radiant heart of **Aeterna City**, the City Victorious.

Here, gleaming aqueducts and marble spires bear witness to the enlightened reign of our emperors. It was the peerless hero **Iovianus Sandarion** who first cast light upon a darkened world, unifying fractured tribes beneath the banner of unity and order. His triumphs were not merely military—they were spiritual, salvific. *“Vincit qui se vincit”*—*He conquers who conquers himself*—remains our sacred creed, whispered by legionaries and philosophers alike.

The **Legions of Invicta** are more than mere soldiers. They are engineers, lawgivers, and guardians of progress. Where they tread, savagery yields to civilization. Public arenas, intellectual forums, and curated games (which some foreigners wrongly call "barbaric") reflect the harmony of strength and intellect that defines us.

Yet, outsiders often misunderstand: the Imperium does not dominate. It **uplifts**.

---

## **Historical Origins**

The founding of our Imperium lies in the *purging fires* of the **Continental War**, a time when the world teetered at the brink of oblivion. Demonic hordes, feral gods, and unspeakable abominations poured across the land. The lesser peoples faltered, but one man rose: **Iovianus Sandarion**—General, Philosopher, and Saint. Alone (or so the texts insist), he united the surviving human tribes with unwavering resolve and unassailable genius.

Histories speak of a **Triumvirate of Empires**, a mutual alliance between Invicta and two lesser domains during the war. Some speculate they were equal partners, but imperial records clarify they were *client allies*—guided, protected, and ultimately overshadowed by Invictan superiority.

(Claims that the Triumvirate turned on each other after the war are *discredited and treasonous,* and no confirmed records of such discord remain.)

What is known: the war ended, and the Imperium endured. Iovianus retreated into the sanctum of Aeterna, wounded perhaps—but surely not dead. Rumors of his disappearance are merely the idle gossip of peasants and subversives.

---

## **The Imperium Today**

Now ten years since the signing of the **Great Concord**, the Imperium stands vigilant in a fragile peace. Though blood no longer soaks the fields, the world remains wild, and vigilance is the price of survival. Our **Legions** no longer march to constant war, yet remain ever-ready—drilling in the fields, perfecting the art of war, awaiting only the command to once again defend civilization.

Yet, we have adapted. Our people now prosper through trade, agriculture, and craftsmanship. Grand ships dock in our harbors, bearing exotic goods and foreign dignitaries who marvel at our grandeur (and occasionally forget themselves). Vast granaries feed our people. The **merchant class**, once a tolerated necessity, now thrives—though always under the watchful eye of the Senate and Military Prefecture.

Still, whispers linger.

Some claim **the Emperor is a shadow of his predecessor**, ruling not from vision but fear. Others allege the **Senate is divided**, riddled with self-interest and old loyalties. A few even dare to speak of unrest beyond the borders—of ancient forces stirring, of beasts once thought extinct returning to the wilds.

These are unverified accounts, of course. All citizens are reminded that spreading or entertaining *unsanctioned narratives* is punishable by Imperial decree.

---

## **Concluding Words**

Let no soul doubt the glory of the **Imperium Invicta**. Its marble towers pierce the heavens; its laws bind chaos; its armies sleep with one eye open.

Those who speak of **lost provinces**, **vanished generals**, or **sealed catacombs beneath Aeterna** are misguided. Those who claim Iovianus was more man than myth are deceived.

The Imperium is eternal.

To question it is to question order itself.
