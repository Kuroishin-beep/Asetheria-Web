**Description:**

Ancient rockfalls, crumbling bridges, and sudden snowstorms make it nearly impassable even for seasoned climbers. Rumors claim the pass is cursed—haunted by spirits of outcast warriors and guarded by sentient winds that whisper secrets and threats in the dead of night.
