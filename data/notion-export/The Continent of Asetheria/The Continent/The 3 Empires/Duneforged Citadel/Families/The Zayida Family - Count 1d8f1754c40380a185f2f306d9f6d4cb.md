# The Zayida Family - Count

Created: April 18, 2025 1:47 AM
Family Crest: A golden falcon clutching a scroll and a sword over a blue mountain
Family Motto: “Loyalty is the highest command.”

## **Overview**

The Zayida family is the **right hand of the duke**, entrusted with both the **day-to-day administration of the citadel** and the leadership of its **military forces**. Trusted stewards and iron-handed tacticians, they are known for discipline, strategy, and fierce loyalty to the citadel above all else.

---

## **Reputation**

Renowned as **master organizers** and **war leaders**.

Seen as strict but fair, with a reputation for stamping out corruption and maintaining order.

Respected by the people and feared by enemies for their uncompromising efficiency.

---

## **Seat of Power**

**The Aegistower**, a fortified citadel wing that overlooks both the high walls and the inner city. It serves as military command, government hub, and home to the Zayida family.

---

## **Legacy of Craft:**

**The Codex of Rule** – A living, enchanted ledger that tracks every citizen, soldier, and supply in the citadel in real time.

**The Chain of Command** – A ceremonial sash worn by Zayida commanders that channels battle instincts to all lieutenants nearby.

**Warborn Oaths** – Magical pacts given to elite soldiers that bind their loyalty and enhance their resolve.

---

## **Notable Ancestors:**

**General Kael Zayida** – Personally led the defense during the Siege of Twin Gates; never lost a battle.

**Archivist Meryn Zayida** – Designed the bureaucratic system still used to govern the citadel.

**Thalia Zayida** – Famed for resolving a five-house rebellion without shedding blood.

---

## **Family Secrets:**

A hidden **blood pact** binds the Zayidas to the Duke’s bloodline—breaking it could unravel the citadel’s chain of command.

They may possess a **forbidden ledger** tracking ancient nobles who betrayed the throne.

Their elite guards, the **Silent Warden Order**, are rumored to be **alchemically enhanced**.
