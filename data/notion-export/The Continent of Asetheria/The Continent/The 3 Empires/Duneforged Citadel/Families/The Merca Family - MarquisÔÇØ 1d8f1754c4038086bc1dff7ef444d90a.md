# The Merca Family - Marquis”

Created: April 17, 2025 9:05 PM
Family Crest: A silver stag standing on a pile of coins, antlers tipped with scales — symbolizing wealth, strength, and balance.
Family Motto: “Trust, like gold, is forged over fire.”

## **Overview**

House Merca is one of the two founding banking families in the citadel and sole builders of the **Mithril Reserve**, the first bank ever constructed in the mountain city. Through shrewd deals and financial foresight, they became the **beating heart of commerce** in the citadel.

---

## **Reputation**

Known as **calculating, clever, and fiercely protective** of their assets and clients.

Beloved by merchants, respected by nobles, and feared by debtors.

Rarely seen wielding weapons—but dangerous in rooms with contracts and coin.

---

## **Seat of Power**

**The Vaulted Court**, an opulent estate built above and within the **Mithril Reserve** itself. Part palace, part high-security financial center.

---

## **Legacy of Craft**

**Runes of Trust** – Magical contracts that burn away when broken and cannot be forged.

**Ledgerlocks** – Enchanted ledgers that only reveal data to blood-bound members or verified heirs.

**The Coinborn Market** – A secret trading chamber said to exist in another realm, accessed through a special coin.

---

## **Notable Ancestors**

**Selvon Merca** – Founded the Mithril Reserve, survived five assassination attempts, and invented the “aether-backed bond.”

**Ysra Merca** – Brokered peace between rival merchant guilds using only three letters and one trade route.

**Gideon Merca** – Said to have bought an entire noble house out of power... and erased them from history.

---

## **Family Secrets**

They secretly **mint their own high-value currency**, accepted only by elite circles.

It’s rumored they hold a **ledger of all secret debts**, including ones the Duke may owe.

They may have **invested in magical espionage**, embedding trackers in high-value artifacts.
