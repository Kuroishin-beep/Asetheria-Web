# Ferratus Vulkrim

Created: July 13, 2025 9:21 PM
Title: Marquis of Vulkrim, Forge-Lord of the Southern Ridges

### **Backstory**

Ferratus was no ordinary noble. Born to a patrician house rumored to have Gaulic blood, he renounced politics to master the forge—an unthinkable move for a man of title. But Ferratus believed that “a true leader must shape more than laws—he must shape steel.”

He established a grand forge-fortress in the mountains east of Rome, near the Dacian frontier. There, under the banner of the Iron Flame, he forged weapons not only for Caesar’s legions but also for a secret order of warrior-smiths loyal only to him.

---

### **Appearance**

- Towering frame, burnished bronze skin from years at the forge.
- Wears a long, scorched cloak fastened with a lion-headed clasp.
- His left hand is encased in an iron gauntlet fused to his skin—a result of an experiment with god-metal (some say meteoric iron, others say stolen divine essence).

---

### **Artifacts Attributed to Him**

**Gladius Aeternum** – A sword said to never dull and drink the heat of battle to grow stronger.

**The Black Anvil** – Still buried deep within the mountain citadel, said to pulse with energy when war approaches.

**The Ferric Crown** – A helm forged with molten silver and volcanic glass, granting visions of future conflict.

---

### **Legacy**

The *Order of Ember*, a secretive brotherhood of warrior-smiths, still worships him as a near-divine figure.

Roman records mention him only in whispers, as a “dangerous eccentric with undue influence over the legions.”

Some claim Caesar himself visited Vulkrim once, seeking a weapon worthy of empire. He never spoke of what he found.
