# The Duke’s Family

Created: April 17, 2025 8:32 PM
Family Crest: A silver-winged helm atop a black mountain, flanked by twin golden sunbursts, shield protecting the mountain.
Family Motto: “We held the peaks. We hold the skies.”

## **Overview**

House **Caerthain**, known widely as *The Caesars*, is the **ruling dynasty** of the citadel and the surrounding mountain dominion. They earned their nobility during the legendary **War of the Continent**, where they **defended the Cloudspine Ridge**—a chain of mountain fortresses—against a coalition of invading empires.

They held **Lake Serathal**, a sacred and mysterious lake atop the highest peak, fending off land, sea, and sky-bound forces with unmatched determination. Their command of **naval mountain ships** and **aerial engineering** has since made them pioneers in **airship development** and strategic dominance.

## **Reputation**

- Universally respected as **guardians of the south** and **champions of survival**.
- Stoic, noble, and occasionally seen as **austere or divine** by the populace.
- Their word is law, and their silence often means more than their decrees.

## **Legacy of Craft & Power**

**Skyward Bastions** – Armored watch-fortresses mounted on cliffs, using early levitation crystals for tactical repositioning.

**The Serathian Fleet** – Sleek, magically-augmented airships that can sail the winds and descend into battle like hawks.

**The Pact** – A treaty signed in enchanted water, binding all allied houses to eternal defense of the empire and its secrets.

## **Notable Ancestors**

**Caesar Kaelen Caerthain**, *The Stormwall* – Led 500 against 50,000 and broke an Acheaoria’s advance and shattered the pass.

**Empress-Lady Vaelis Caerthain** – Founded and invented the navy and was said to walk on air during her final campaign.

**Prince Deyric Caerthain** – Vanished into the lake on a ship of starlight and was never seen again. Some say he speaks to current rulers in dreams.

## **Family Secrets**

The creatures in **Lake** are not just guarded—they are **worshipped** by some factions within the family.

The lake is said to house a **time-anchored being** or entity that grants visions, power... or madness.

House Caerthain may not be fully human anymore—*the bloodline is said to have changed* after centuries of exposure to the lake’s magic.

A **prototype airship**, the *Sovereign Star.*
