# The Vulkrim Family - Marquis

Created: April 17, 2025 9:48 PM
Family Crest: A molten hammer over crossed tongs, ringed by a crown of fire
Family Motto: “From fire, we rise.”

## **Overview**

House Vulkrim was born in flame and forged through grit. Originally common blacksmiths, their skill earned them friendship with the Duke himself, and eventually **noble status**. Now one of the **Three Great Smithing Houses**, their work arms the citadel’s elite and adorns its greatest halls.=

---

## **Reputation**

- Celebrated as **legendary smiths** who rival even dwarven work.
- Humble in origin but proud in bearing, they earn respect rather than demand it.
- Their forges are considered sacred ground by artisans and warriors alike.

---

## **Seat of Power**

**The Geomanthic Foundries** carved into a volcanic ridge near the citadel base. Part fortress, part forge complex, and always burning.

---

## **Legacy of Craft:**

**The Embersteel Line** – A metal alloy that glows with heat yet never burns the wielder, forged exclusively by House Vulkrim.

**Living Anvils** – Sentient anvils that advise smiths through runic hums and flashes.

**The Titanplate Commission** – War armor forged for giants during the Stormbound Era.

---

## **Notable Ancestors:**

**Gorran Vulkrim** – Founder, whose bare hands once twisted a steel beam during a collapsing mine rescue.

**Velda Vulkrim** – Forged the **Shield of Tenfold Defiance**, which turned a battle single-handedly.

**Erkan Vulkrim** – Crafted a masterpiece blade gifted to the Duke, said to sing when danger nears.

---

## **Family Secrets:**

A **hidden forgefire**, believed to be a shard of elemental flame, fuels their greatest works.

There’s a forbidden design in their archives—**a soulbound forge armor**, capable of merging with its wearer permanently.

The Duke’s original sword? **It wasn’t forged... it was repaired**—suggesting it came from something far older.
