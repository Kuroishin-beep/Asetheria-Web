## History

---

Born and raised on a farm near Goldenfields, Kate joined the Pelor monastery as a teen. She never excelled in her studies but was devout and had a natural aptitude for divine magic. 

In her 20s she was hired to be a healer for a group of adventures. She spent a few years fighting gnolls and uncovering treasure. She fell in love with one of her fellow adventures, after her lover was killed in battle she asked to be reassigned. 

She was sent to the church in Reid's Ferry. It was meant to be a temporary assignment but she fell in love with the town and its people and has stayed ever since. 

## Personality

---

**Ideal:** Greater good. She is willing to lay down her life in defense of others.

**Flaw:** Fire is her answer for almost any challenge.
