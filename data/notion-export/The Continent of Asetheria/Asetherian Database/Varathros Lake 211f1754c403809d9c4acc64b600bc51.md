# Varathros Lake

Tags: Great Lake

## **GM Summary: Varathros Lake**

**Overview:**

Varathros Lake is a massive, mysterious inland sea forged from destruction. Once a small, serene lake, it was catastrophically reshaped during the **Continental War of Asetheria**, a conflict of immense magical and martial devastation. A massive sinkhole tore the land asunder, filling over centuries with rainwater and flooding subterranean caverns. It is now a deep, unpredictable, and dangerous body of water, whose very tides seem to remember the war that birthed it.

---

## REGION I: **Northern Crest – The Neutral City**

### Overview:

This is the most *civilized* and *stable* part of Varathros Lake. From the coast up to 50 nautical miles, the waters remain relatively safe. Past that distance, however, the lake becomes unpredictable with rough currents and spiraling wave troughs. This buffer zone allowed ancient civilizations to thrive—now reduced to coastal outposts and submerged ruins.

### Points of Interest:

- **Deiperdeum, the Neutral City-State**
    
    Once a naval power before the Continental War, now an autonomous fortress-city ruled by a Senate of Mages and Admirals. They regulate lake travel with iron-handed bureaucracy.
    
    **Quirk**: A glowing dome of gray fog covers the city’s harbor, never dissipating, rumored to be the lingering breath of a drowned god.
    
- **Alexandria Sanctum**
    
    A small, rocky islet with a titanic lighthouse said to be carved from sky-glass. It hums during certain lunar alignments. Sailors say it sings to the stars.
    
    Some claim it was never built—only revealed.
    
- **Kindred Depths**
    
    An underwater reef and cave system home to a confederation of aquatic races (e.g., Sea Elves, Tritons, Water Genasi). They speak of **the Scaled Accord**, a pact made with peaceful dragons.
    
    These **dragon guardians**—ancient and wise—rest coiled around ancient ruins, keeping secrets of the war locked away.
    

### Mystical Element:

The waters here remember battle. When the moon is high, **phantoms of ancient naval clashes** appear, replaying in silent, spectral reenactments. Interacting with them could unveil hidden truths… or draw attention from something older.

## REGION II: **The Arcane Maw – Middle Expanse**

### Overview:

The true heart of the lake. Calm one day, writhing the next. Storms swirl without wind, and currents shift as if aware of the ships that ride them. Magic here is *amplified*, but so too is unpredictability.

### The Lone Isle:

- **Isle of Oracles** (also known as *Mount Zharion*)
    
    A towering, rocky island with a spired mountain. Atop stands a **“lighthouse”** whose light pulses in strange colors—actually the **Arcane Sanctum of Archmage Vaelithar**.
    
    Vaelithar is rarely seen, but their apprentices speak in riddles and make offerings to the lake.
    
- Temporal Rifts
    
    Sometimes, ships that enter this region return aged decades… or arrive before they left.
    
    Some wizards believe the lake’s waters contain fragments of time, suspended like silt.
    
- Eddies of Memory
    
    Swirling water vortexes that can steal or implant *memories* of people, locations, or even forgotten spells. They may also reveal glimpses of the war or of the creature below.
    
- The Silver Reefs
    
    Submerged spires of magical crystal known to warp spells cast nearby. Arcane items brought near them sometimes *speak* or pulse. Some believe the reefs are the fossilized remains of fallen war titans.
    

### Mystical Element:

The air buzzes with unseen runes. Spells cast here may backfire—or evolve into greater forms. *Magic itself seems curious here.*

## 🕊️ REGION III: **Abssal Ring – Nemegrath’s Halo**

### Overview:

A ring-shaped trench around the central deeps, home to sunken ruins, overgrown cities, and **temples to drowned gods**. The water grows darker, colder. Some say the sea gods reside here—not just in presence, but in **architecture**.

### Notable Locales:

- **The Vaulted Deepdomes**
    
    Crystalline ruins of a forgotten empire that once worshipped the **gods of tides, moons, and secrets**.
    
    Creatures of divine heritage—sea celestial hybrids, forgotten demigods—guard the thresholds.
    
- **Temple of the Undrowned King**
    
    A spiraling column of coral and petrified bone. Only those who **hold their breath for an hour** may enter. Inside, **liquid gravity** flows upward, and **statues weep brine** that induces visions.
    
- **Nemegrath’s Eye**
    
    A vast obsidian iris embedded in the lakebed, always open. If stared into, players might witness flashes of **divine betrayal**, **war crimes**, or **the forging of the chains of Ezerantheil**.
    

### Mystical Element:

The water here **rejects divination** and **alters language**. Prayers spoken aloud turn to foreign tongues. Writing in journals becomes jumbled, often referencing **things the writer doesn’t remember knowing**.

### REGION IV: **The Hadal Deeps – The Ebon Delve**

### Overview:

The bottomless trench at the lake’s heart. Not even the gods claim this depth. A void in magic and time, where light drowns, and reality becomes brittle.

### Anchored Horror:

- **Ezerantheil the Unblinking**
    
    Once a prophetic titan or godlike entity that saw the *threads of fate* as one sees branches in a forest. Feared by all sides in the Continental War, for he predicted the end of empires and the fall of gods.
    
    **Bound in chains of star-iron**, left to rot in silence. He is said to speak now only through dreams, rain patterns, or broken machinery.
    
    Legends claim:
    
    - He has **no eyes**, but sees all.
    - He cannot move, yet **his presence deepens** every year.
    - He speaks only in **truths, but twisted by madness**.

### Points of Dread:

- **The Silt Cathedral**
    
    A buried structure flickering between existence and not. Those who enter vanish or return weeks later, *with pale eyes and new names*.
    
- **Chainfall**
    
    The location where **the star-iron chains breach the trench ceiling**, glowing with dim divine energy. Some adventurers seek these for their unbreakable power. Few return.
    
- **Echoing Abyss**
    
    Even speaking here is strange. Words stretch for minutes. Sound loops. Voices return altered.
    

### Mystical Element:

This is not just the lake’s depth. It is its **soul**—its **prison**—its **origin**. The war didn’t make this trench. The trench *was always there*, waiting.

### **Imperium Invicta** (Roman-themed)

> “We tamed the lake. And where we did not, we chained it.”
> 
> 
> — Imperial Historian Braccus Valente
> 

### Their Claim:

- The Imperium insists **they won the Battle of the Broken Masts**, the greatest naval battle during the war, held on Varathros’ northern tides.
- They built **floating fortresses** and **subaquatic legions** to subdue deep creatures.
- Tiberus, the mighty river that runs from Varathros to their heartlands, is “blessed by Mars, the god of war.”

### The Hidden Truths:

- Archaeological logs mention they *lost* the Battle of the Broken Masts horrifically—half the fleet vanished into a whirlpool.
- Their famed “floating fortresses” were unfinished prototypes. One capsized. Survivors were *erased* from record.
- The Tiberius sometimes runs *red with algae* after storms—interpreted as a sign from “gods,” but it’s likely environmental runoff.

### What They Claim:

- **"We crushed the Hellenorian fleet at the Battle of the Broken Masts."**
    
    *Official doctrine.* Monuments in Aquae Redavia show a victorious Imperial general standing atop a shattered trireme.
    
- **"Our ironclad 'Tritonis-class' galleons never sank—invincible even to the storms."**
    
    Mentioned in military treatises as technological marvels. No verified wreckage ever found.
    

### 🤫 What Others Whisper:

- The **Battle of the Broken Masts** was a **disastrous loss** — their admiral reportedly **went mad**, and the flagship *Domina Aeternum* was **swallowed whole** by a rogue wave.
- **Half the fleet was sabotaged internally**—by a now-expunged house that worshipped “the thing beneath.”
- The "invincible" ironclads? **One caught fire on launch. One rusted through. The third was never finished.** The name was reused on merchant logs to give the illusion of deployment.

### **Hellenoria** (Greek-themed)

> “Varathros is not a lake. It is a living myth. A divine labyrinth made of salt and sorrow.”
> 
> 
> — Priestess Odyma, Oracle of Crythonis
> 

### Their Claim:

- Hellenoria tells of **the Isles of Prophecy**, floating islands where demigods were born from lake foam.
- Their **navy of triremes** once “pushed back the dark,” banishing the storm-mages of Acheaoria.
- **Crythonis River**, flowing from the lake, is “the last breath of Thalassene, goddess of deep truths.”

### The Hidden Truths:

- The “Isles of Prophecy” may have been **illusions** caused by arcane interference.
- Their trireme fleet was indeed formidable—but **a Hellenorian general defected** during the war and helped create the chain that bound the creature below.
- Crythonis was once used as a waste channel for failed divination rites.

- **"We brought the storm down ourselves and swept the Invictans from the lake!"**
    
    Their war hymns celebrate the Stormcallers of Crythonis, said to have conjured a divine tempest to break the Imperium line.
    
- **"Our Seafather-General rode a hippocampus across the lake and never lost a battle."**
    
    There's even a fresco in the Amphitheater of Echoes depicting it.
    

### **Acheaoria** (Persian-themed, distant empire)

> “Varathros is the cradle of secrets. We do not conquer it. We observe it.”
> 
> 
> — Namazahrin, the Mirror-Binder of the Sapphire Archive
> 

### Their Claim:

- Acheaoria claims they never sought to control the lake, only study it.
- They possess maps that accurately predict every storm and current (they don’t share them).
- The river Zaremesh flows *backwards* during eclipses—interpreted as divine alignment.

### The Hidden Truths:

- Some say Acheaoria tried to **raise the creature beneath Varathros** as a weapon—but lost control.
- Their maps may be **forgeries**, made to appear prophetic after the fact.
- Zaremesh’s reversal is not divine—it may be a **subterranean backflow** from a massive sinkhole network.

### What They Claim:

- **"We had no interest in conquest. We merely observed. The others fought blindly."**
    
    Imperial scholars state they remained neutral and used the chaos to **map leyline shifts**.
    
- **"Our ships could outpace all others using windless sails—divine cloth that harnessed memory."**
    
    Allegedly blessed by the Moon-Binders of the Sapphire Archive.
    

### 🕳️ What Lurks Beneath:

- They fielded a **secret navy of obsidian hulls**—stealth ships that vanished in daylight. **None were ever seen again.**
- Their "neutrality" may have been a **cover** for a failed attempt to **summon or awaken** the being chained in the Hadal Deep.
- The **windless sails** did exist, but were unstable — reports claim the **memories stored in them corrupted the minds of their crews**, causing mutinies and drownings mid-lake.
