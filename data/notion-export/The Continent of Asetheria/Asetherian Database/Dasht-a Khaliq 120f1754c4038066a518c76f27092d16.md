# **Dasht a Khaliq** *(The Divine Wastes)*

**Biome**: High fantasy desert wasteland with divine-tainted zones

**Continent**: Eastern third of Asetheria

**Dominant Power**: **Acheaoria Empire** (Ancient Persian-inspired)

**Primary Threats**: Environmental hazards, corrupted beings, undead, desert raiders, and spirit-beasts

---

## 💡 GM Overview

**Use this desert as:**

- A crucible for survival-based campaigns
- A high-stakes setting for political intrigue between empires
- A divine or cosmic horror-infused sandbox
- A gateway into ancient ruins and forgotten god-corpses

**Core Themes**:

☀ Divine aftermaths and corruption

🌵 Harsh terrain and survival

⚔ Imperial politics vs creeping entropy

🌀 Hidden magic beneath ancient sands

## 🗺️ Regional Breakdown for GMs

### 🜁 REGION I: **Zahr al-Mawt** – *“Blossom of Death”*

**(Northeast - Border of the Dead Realms)**

- **Primary Tone**: Survival horror / Cosmic corruption
- **Biome**: Tainted dunes, dark sands, rotting winds
- **Environmental Effect**: *All healing spells halve their effectiveness unless divine (e.g., from gods of life or purity)*

### 🔥 Threats

- **Rot-Beasts**: Mutated animals and divine leftovers
- **Death-Marches**: Undead processions heading aimlessly into the dunes
- **Blight Titans**: Semi-sentient decaying god-husks
- **Blasphemous Weather**: Acid winds, bone storms, voice-tempests (whispering storms)

### 🧭 Adventure Hooks

- A cult seeks to awaken a forgotten deity buried in the sands
- An imperial fort has gone dark — overrun or converted?
- A god’s ribcage now serves as the lair of a crystal dracolich

### 🎲 Encounter Types

| Encounter | CR Range | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rot-Lions | 3–5 | Pack hunters, bone-covered beasts |
| Titanspawn | 7–10 | Aberrant giants with memory echo attacks |
| Wandering Legion | Variable | Undead soldiers in full Acheaorian gear |

### 🜂 REGION II: **Ruhaniin Vale** – *“Spirits’ Rest”*

**(Central / Eastern – Civilized Desert Core)**

- **Primary Tone**: Political intrigue / survival civilization
- **Biome**: Semi-arid zones, oases, underground cities
- **Environmental Effect**: *Random mirage effects trigger every long rest without a Sahari guide*

### 🏛 Points of Interest

- **Kharnesh** – Capital city atop the Eye of Havar, layered, vertical city with water-based caste politics
- **The Under-Sands** – Series of sunken towns connected by tunnels and whispering passageways
- **Vault of Silt** – Magical aqueduct hub and leyline stabilizer guarded by mages

### 🧭 Adventure Hooks

- Tensions rise between water-rich and water-starved towns: spark of civil conflict
- A noble house has secretly made a pact with desert elementals to overthrow the ruling Shah
- An underground city has broken contact – recent tremors awakened something beneath

### 🎲 Encounter Types

| Encounter | CR Range | Notes |

| Sand Raiders (Sandsworn) | 2–6 | Ride dune-serpents, use mirage illusions |
| Mirage Beasts | 5–8 | Phantasmal predators that only become real when seen |
| Political intrigue | N/A | Espionage, assassination attempts, court trials |

### 🜃 REGION III: **Khamsin’s Reach** – *“Breath of the Desert”*

**(Southeast – Wild Frontier)**

- **Primary Tone**: Frontier mystery / expansion tension
- **Biome**: Rocky desert, petrified forests, creeping sands
- **Environmental Effect**: *Every dawn roll 1d6 – on a 1, a new 10-mile stretch is swallowed by sand*

### 🏛 Points of Interest

- **Petra of the South** – City carved into living rock, oasis slowly drying
- **The Shifting Pines** – Petrified woodlands with dryad-lich remnants
- **Stone Choir** – Desert obelisks that sing in wind, ancient weather-control relics

### 🧭 Adventure Hooks

- A region of forest disappears overnight, buried by sudden dunes
- A druidic circle battles a sand elemental titan that awakens each solstice
- A ruined observatory atop a plateau hides weather-warping rituals

Scattered across the southern sands are **floating obelisks** and **half-sunken monoliths**, covered in glyphs that no scholar can fully decipher. Every few years, a new one appears. None have seen them rise. But those who touch them… change. Some speak in tongues not heard since the First Epoch. Others disappear into the dunes without a trace.

Worst of all, Khamsin’s Edge feels like it’s pushing forward. The dunes inch farther into neighboring lands with each season. Trees turn to salt. The air grows heavier. The Empire sends sandbreakers to stop the drift, but the desert always reclaims.

| Sandwights | 6–8 | Undead that swim beneath sand and whisper truths |

## ⚖️ Faction Hooks

### **The Acheaorian Empire**

- Seeks to reclaim ancient cities buried beneath the sands
- Employs Sahari (elite desert rangers) to guard trade and patrol desert zones
- Water control is political power
- Hosts multiple **noble houses** vying for influence over water rights and border defense

### **The Sahari**

- Elite desertborn nomads, mystical guides, and survivalists
- Neutral in politics but hold spiritual and logistical power
- Have knowledge of "The Sand Beneath the Sand" — secret tunnels to ancient epochs

### **Cult of the Pale Sun**

- Worship a dead solar god buried in the Zahr al-Mawt
- Believe the desert is the only “pure” land unspoiled by civilization
- Plot to cause droughts and awaken god-corpses for purification

## 🔧 Mechanical Features to Use

- **Travel Fatigue Tables**: Include dehydration rolls, mirage hallucination checks, and sandstorm navigational DCs.
- **Mirage Encounters**: At any moment, roll for a false oasis or a projected village. A DC 15 Wisdom (Survival) or (Insight) may detect the illusion.
- **Living Sands**: Treat desert terrain as potentially mobile. Use shifting battle maps or random terrain changes.
- **Sand Rot**: Curse/disease spread by touching divine-corrupted sands (WIS saves, permanent mutations or delusions).

---

## 🧩 Magic and Relics

- **The Obsidian Compass** – Points not to north, but to the last source of divine tears (used to find hidden oases or holy relics)
- **Scrolls of Sand-Tongue** – Let casters speak to the wind, revealing buried ruins or avoiding storms
- **Sunforged Glassblade** – Created from godlight-scorched sand; glows when undead or titanic remnants are near
