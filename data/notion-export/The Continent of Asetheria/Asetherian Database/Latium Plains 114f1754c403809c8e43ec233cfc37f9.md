# Latium Plains

Tags: Plains

## **“Praetor’s Fall” – The Field That Knelt**

*As documented by Scribe Pellion Meraquas, Aeterna Lexicon*

*Entry filed under: Topographies of Significance, Imperial Censure Tier II*

---

> “The wind still bows when it passes here. Something greater once stood upon this soil… and something older made it kneel.”
> 
> 
> — *From the banned chronicle “Whispers of the Earth”*
> 

To the **east of Campania Veta**, past vineyards too well-tended to be natural and orchards whose fruits never rot, lies a vast depression in the earth—**a field that slopes inward like a bowl**, unnaturally smooth, unnervingly quiet. Locals call it **Praetor’s Fall**, though no records confirm which praetor—if any—ever commanded legions there.

The plain dips for nearly a mile, forming a **shallow crater** too symmetrical to be natural, too clean to be accidental. Some scholars argue that it is the remains of an arcane bombardment, or a ritual collapse. Others—namely the few who have spent the night there—claim the land itself moved, as if **kneeling in submission** to an unseen force.

---

- **The Heretic-Praetor Thesis:** One school of thought states that **Praetor Lurentius Varro**, a proud and reckless general, attempted to declare himself **"Imperator-Aeternum"** during a military campaign in the outer provinces. As he performed a forbidden rite meant to elevate his soul into godhood, the land rejected him. The very ground beneath his army bowed—and then swallowed them.
    - *Only their banners were recovered, half-buried in obsidian dust.*
- **The Sleeping Titan Theory:** A conflicting myth claims no man ever caused the Fall. Instead, the depression is the **resting place of a colossal being**—a god, a giant, a primordial engine of war. The slope is simply the curve of its back beneath the earth. Nightmares suffered by travelers seem to depict a ribcage beneath soil.
- **Architectural Anomaly:** Some claim the land below is **hollow**, and that ruins—perfectly preserved and older than the Imperium—sit beneath layers of soft earth. The **obsidian monolith** at the crater’s heart, called simply **“The Witness”**, is believed to be a keystone, anchor, or eye.

---

### **Physical Traits of the Region**

The wind within Praetor’s Fall never follows the direction of the larger plains. It moves in **gentle spirals**, as though circling an absent drain.

**The Soil:** Some report that footprints vanish seconds after being made—others that they deepen over time until they resemble grave markers.

**The Witness (Obsidian Pillar):**

- 12 feet tall. Smooth. Warm.
- Emits a low hum when touched with a bare hand, particularly during eclipses or storms.
- Some claim it **records thoughts**, offering visions not of the past or future, but **possibilities never chosen**.
- One scholar, **Archivist Doria Vem**, reported seeing herself kneeling before an altar. She disappeared three days later. Her sandals were found beside the pillar, filled with dry ash.

---

> “We measured the Fall by day. By nightfall, the measurements had doubled. The next morning, the field was back to its old shape. But our shadows were still long.”
> 
> 
> — *Excerpt from the journal of Legion Surveyor Valcis Grane*
> 

> “I heard chanting from beneath my feet. And I was alone.”
> 
> 
> — *Campfire tale, attributed to an unnamed merchant*
> 

> “No birds fly over it. No worms burrow through it. And when you sleep in Praetor’s Fall, your dreams are not your own.”
> 
> 
> — *Old soldier’s warning, scratched into a barracks wall near Campania’s eastern road*
> 

---

 A fringe sect worships the Fall itself, believing it is a **place of rebirth**. Most who enter seeking enlightenment return broken… or don’t return at all.

Time moves strangely here. One traveler reports spending “an hour” within the Fall—yet emerged five days later, with a beard and memory gaps.

The Empire denies all anomalies in the region. A legion detachment once stationed nearby was quietly reassigned and the road redirected to bypass it. Scribes accessing historical documents about Praetor’s Fall require a Ciphered Seal of Red Marble, issued only to members of the 4th Circle or higher.

---

> “If the land could pray, this is what the prayer would look like.”
> 
> 
> — *Scribe Pellion Meraquas, after returning from the Fall, never to write again*
>
