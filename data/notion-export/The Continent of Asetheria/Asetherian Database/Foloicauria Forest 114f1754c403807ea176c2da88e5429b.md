# Foloicauria Forest

Tags: Great Forest

### GM Summary: **Foloicaria, the Living Wilds**

Foloicaria is a vast, ancient forest untouched by the millennium-long Continental War. Its thick canopies and dense undergrowth pulse with pure, unfiltered Nature Magic, often called "Vitalous Energy." This saturation has caused rampant, often unpredictable mutations among flora and fauna—creatures twisted not into abominations, but into avatars of nature’s primal will. The forest is very much alive, and many believe it to be sentient.

Three regions define Foloicaria:

1. **The Eastern Peaks – Mount Vaeltheruun:** A lone, fog-shrouded mountain visible from afar. Whispers say its summit reveals a colossal serpentine silhouette. Here reside **Echidna and Typhon**, progenitors of all monsters. They rule the eastern wilds, and their monstrous descendants roam freely under their watchful gaze.
2. **The Heartgrove – Wild Elf Domain:** In the central region lies a single city, founded by Elven clans who braved the unknown ages ago. Over centuries, their forms have adapted—some growing fur, feathers, or bark-like skin—blending into the ecosystem. They are now known as **Wild Elves**, protectors and stewards of the forest’s middle domain.
3. **The Western Wilds – Medusan Territory:** The west is home to **Medusa**, who formed an alliance with fierce **Amazonian tribes**. These warrior-women and serpentine guardians live in harmony, mastering the land’s dangers and reshaping it into a domain of trial, challenge, and survival.

Even the strongest warlords and archmages of the empires avoid Foloicaria. Its creatures are beyond cataloging, its geography seems to shift, and ancient, slumbering powers lie dormant—or watchful.
