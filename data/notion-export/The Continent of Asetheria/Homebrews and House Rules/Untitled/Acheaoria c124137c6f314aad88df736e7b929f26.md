Created: October 16, 2024 11:15 AM

### **Benefit Options:**

1. **Endurance of the Sands** – You can forgo sleep for up to 12 hours without suffering exhaustion. This can only be done once between long rests.
2. **Survivor’s Resilience** – You gain advantage on Constitution saving throws, helping you endure physically taxing situations.
3. **Veil of Sand** – While in familiar terrain, you can use your bonus action to hide in lightly obscured areas, making you harder to detect.

### **Drawback Options:**

1. **Harsh Life** – Healing from spells or potions is reduced by 2 points due to the toll your upbringing has taken on your body.
2. **Cautious Demeanor** – Once per encounter, you can only take the Dash, Disengage, Hide, or Dodge action on your turn, limiting your offensive options temporarily.
3. **Tempered Feet** – When in cold or wet terrain, your movement speed is reduced by 10 feet as your body struggles to adapt to the conditions.
