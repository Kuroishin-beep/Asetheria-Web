# Hellenoria

Created: October 16, 2024 11:15 AM

### **Benefit Options:**

1. **Arcane Insight** – Once per long rest, you can cast *Detect Magic* without expending a spell slot or requiring components.
2. **Cultural Empathy** – You gain proficiency in one additional language and advantage on Intelligence checks related to cultural knowledge. Additionally, you have advantage on Charisma checks when interacting with fellow Hellenorians.
3. **Philosopher’s Insight** – Once per short or long rest, you can grant yourself advantage on an Intelligence check or saving throw, reflecting your ability to analyze situations carefully.

### **Drawback Options:**

1. **Arcanic Overthinker** – If you choose the **Arcane Insight** benefit, you cannot cast spells or concentrate on spells during the first round of combat as your mind races through possibilities.
2. **Skeptical Nature** – You cannot gain Inspiration from allies unless you have spent at least 6 months in-game with them or had a meaningful bonding moment (as determined by the DM).
3. **Overanalyzing** – During the first turn of combat, your movement speed is reduced to 0 as you hesitate to act, trying to assess the situation perfectly.
