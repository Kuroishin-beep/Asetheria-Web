# Imperium Invicta

Created: October 16, 2024 11:15 AM

### **Benefit Options:**

1. **Martial Training** – You gain proficiency with one martial weapon (e.g., longsword, battleaxe) and medium armor. This allows you to engage in combat more effectively.
2. **Tactical Mind** – You gain a +2 bonus to initiative rolls, helping you react quickly and take advantage in battle.
3. **Battle Formation** – If wielding a shield, you can take the Dash action as a bonus action at the start of combat.

### **Drawback Options:**

1. **Rigid Views** – When encountering unforeseen circumstances, you must succeed on a DC 10 Intelligence saving throw. On a failure, you are unable to act for one turn due to indecision.
2. **Programmed Conquerors** – If you select the **Battle Formation** benefit, your movement speed is halved while retreating from combat, as your tactical instincts conflict with the idea of withdrawal.
3. **Enforced Brutality** – Due to your militaristic upbringing, you cannot deal non-lethal damage. Any attack that reduces a creature to 0 hit points results in its death.
