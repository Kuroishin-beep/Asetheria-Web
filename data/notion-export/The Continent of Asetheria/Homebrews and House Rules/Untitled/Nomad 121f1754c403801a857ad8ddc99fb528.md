# Nomad

Created: October 16, 2024 11:18 AM

### **Benefit Options:**

1. **Urban Adaptability** – You gain advantage on Intelligence checks to find information (locations, rumors, etc.) in urban environments, helping you navigate cities easily.
2. **Nomadic Insight** – You have advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks while moving through urban areas, reflecting your ability to blend in and avoid unwanted attention.
3. **Traveler’s Charm** – You gain advantage on checks involving haggling or negotiation in markets, improving your ability to barter for goods and services.

### **Drawback Options:**

1. **Rootless Existence** – You cannot gain the benefits of friendship or allies until you’ve spent at least one in-game month with a group, making it harder to build trust.
2. **Isolation** – You cannot benefit from the Help action during social encounters unless you and the assisting character agree (in-character and with the DM) that a bond of trust has formed.
3. **Wandering Heart** – If you remain in the same settlement for more than a month, you suffer a -1 penalty to Wisdom (Perception) checks until you leave, as your restless nature affects your focus.
