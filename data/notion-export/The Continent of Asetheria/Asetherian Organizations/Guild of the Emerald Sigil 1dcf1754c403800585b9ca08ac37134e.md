# Guild of the Emerald Sigil

Dedicated To: Adamus
Organization: Titanic Organization
Subcategory: The Virtuous
Type: Artisans

Artisans of the Emerald Sigil carve symbols into life, embedding divine intention into every design. Their work, both functional and beautiful, channels the orderly vision of Adamus. Each sigil is a whisper of balance etched in stone, wood, and glass.
