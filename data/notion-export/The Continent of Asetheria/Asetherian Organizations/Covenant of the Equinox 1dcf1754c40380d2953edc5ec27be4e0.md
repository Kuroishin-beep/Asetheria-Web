# Covenant of the Equinox

Dedicated To: Eos
Organization: Titanic Organization
Subcategory: The Impartial
Type: Druidic Circle

This druidic circle honors the delicate tension between light and dark, ever walking the line of celestial balance. Under the guidance of Eos, they observe the world’s transitions—dawn, dusk, death, and rebirth. In the moment between moments, they find truth.
