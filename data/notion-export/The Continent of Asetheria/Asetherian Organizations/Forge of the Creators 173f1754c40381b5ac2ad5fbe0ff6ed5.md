# Forge of the Creators

Dedicated To: Ophion
Organization: Titanic Organization
Subcategory: The Virtuous
Type: Secret Society

Within this secretive enclave, divine sparks ignite invention, and every creation is a prayer to Ophion. The Forge of the Creators melds magic and metal to reshape the world, one artifact at a time. They are sculptors of destiny, hammering gods’ dreams into form.
