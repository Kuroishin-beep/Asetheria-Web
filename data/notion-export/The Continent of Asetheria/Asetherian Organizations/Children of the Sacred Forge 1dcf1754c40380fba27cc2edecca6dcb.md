# Children of the Sacred Forge

Dedicated To: Hyperion
Organization: Titanic Organization
Subcategory: The Virtuous
Type: Artisans

Bound by the legacy of Hyperion, the Children of the Sacred Forge shape flame and form with reverence. Their craft is steeped in tradition, where every anvil strike echoes with celestial memory. They do not just build—they awaken what lies within.
