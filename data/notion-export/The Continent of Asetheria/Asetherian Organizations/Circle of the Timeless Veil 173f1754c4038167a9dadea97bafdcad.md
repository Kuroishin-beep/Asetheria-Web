# Circle of the Timeless Veil

Dedicated To: Ostasus
Organization: Titanic Organization
Subcategory: The Impartial
Type: Secret Society

The Circle of the Timeless Veil seeks truths lost to memory and shrouded in ages past. Hidden behind layers of ritual and time, they pursue the eternal patterns that guide fate. They are watchers at the edge of now, with eyes that never blink.
