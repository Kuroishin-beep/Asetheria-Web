# Hammer of Lumina

Dedicated To: Helios
Organization: Titanic Organization
Subcategory: The Virtuous
Type: Forging

The Hammer of Lumina is a sacred forge where sunlight becomes steel, and honor is tempered in celestial fire. Revering Helios, its artisans labor unseen to craft weapons of radiant justice. Their brilliance is hidden—but their works will one day shine.
