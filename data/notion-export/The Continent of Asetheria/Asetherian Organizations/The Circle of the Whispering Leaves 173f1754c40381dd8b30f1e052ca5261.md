# The Circle of the Whispering Leaves

Dedicated To: Nature and Harmony
Organization: Continental Organization
Subcategory: The Virtuous
Type: Religious Order

Deep within glades untouched by time, this religious order communes with nature’s quiet truths. They believe harmony is found in stillness, in rustling leaves and drifting pollen. Their rituals breathe with the forest, rooted in the divine rhythm of life itself.
