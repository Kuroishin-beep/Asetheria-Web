# Mithradaic Cult

Dedicated To: Strength and Brotherhood
Organization: Continental Organization
Subcategory: The Virtuous
Type: Religious Order

Bound by oaths of strength and unbreakable camaraderie, the Mithradaic Cult forges warriors of both body and soul. They blend spiritual fervor with martial rigor, revering the divine through acts of endurance and sacrifice. Brotherhood is their altar, and pain their prayer.
