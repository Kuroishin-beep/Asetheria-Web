# The Crimson Sigil of the Shattered Spear

Dedicated To: Ares
Organization: Hellenorian Organization
Subcategory: The Impartial
Type: Military Order

This military order thrives on conflict and controlled destruction, wielding broken symbolism as a source of strength. They turn loss into fuel for fury and broken blades into unyielding resolve. War is not just their trade—it is their creed.
