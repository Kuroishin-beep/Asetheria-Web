# Arcane Veil of Mysteries

Dedicated To: Trivia
Organization: Invictian Organization
Subcategory: The Occult
Type: Secret Society

A cloaked society of spellcasters, the Arcane Veil explores lost knowledge and veiled truths. They speak in riddles, act through proxies, and thrive in the forgotten corners of the arcane. Their existence is denied by many—and feared by more.
