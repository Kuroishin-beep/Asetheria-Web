# The Howling Bell

Dedicated To: Selene
Organization: Titanic Organization
Subcategory: The Occult
Type: Bardic College

The Howling Bell is a bardic college whose songs pierce the veil, stirring ancient nightmares and divine echoes. Under Selene’s eerie moon, they sing truths too terrible for silence. Their melodies linger like phantom winds—unsettling, beautiful, and impossible to forge
