# The Collegium of the Sacred Sight

Dedicated To: Divination and Prophecy
Organization: Continental Organization
Subcategory: The Virtuous
Type: Magical Society

This esteemed magical society devotes itself to the study of prophecy, reading omens in smoke, stars, and silence. Within its halls, seers cultivate visions of futures not yet born. To walk its corridors is to glimpse tomorrow—whether you're ready or not.
