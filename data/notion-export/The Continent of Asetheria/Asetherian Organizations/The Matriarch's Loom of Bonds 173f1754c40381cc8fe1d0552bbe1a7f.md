# The Matriarch's Loom of Bonds

Dedicated To: Hera
Organization: Hellenorian Organization
Subcategory: The Impartial
Type: Family/Tribe

This family-based society weaves kinship and duty into a sacred structure upheld by generations of matriarchs. They see relationships as threads in a divine tapestry, each choice shaping legacy. Their influence is subtle but deeply rooted in tradition.
