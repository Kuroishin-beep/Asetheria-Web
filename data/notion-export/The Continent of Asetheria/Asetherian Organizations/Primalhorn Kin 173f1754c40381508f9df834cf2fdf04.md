# Primalhorn Kin

Dedicated To: Pan
Organization: Hellenorian Organization
Subcategory: The Impartial
Type: Family/Tribe

The Primalhorn Kin are a tribal family bound by ancestral rituals and a deep connection to the wild. Their lineage is marked by horns worn with pride and strength drawn from untamed lands. They are both feared and respected for their primal heritage.
