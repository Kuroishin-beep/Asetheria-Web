# Tranquil Hand Enclave

Dedicated To: Terra
Organization: Hellenorian Organization, Invictian Organization
Subcategory: The Impartial
Type: Druidic Circle

The Tranquil Hand Enclave is a union of druids from multiple traditions who practice healing and restoration. They serve as mediators between nature and civilization, often calming conflicts with serene wisdom. Their work brings quiet peace where it is needed most.
