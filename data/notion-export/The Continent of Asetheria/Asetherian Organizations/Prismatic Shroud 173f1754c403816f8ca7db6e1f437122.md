# Prismatic Shroud

Dedicated To: Arcus
Organization: Invictian Organization
Subcategory: The Occult
Type: Crime Syndicate

The Prismatic Shroud is a cunning crime syndicate that uses illusion and misdirection as its weapons of choice. Their operations are known to the public, yet the truth remains cloaked in ever-shifting identities. From smuggling to subtle manipulations, their reach is vast but never fully seen.
