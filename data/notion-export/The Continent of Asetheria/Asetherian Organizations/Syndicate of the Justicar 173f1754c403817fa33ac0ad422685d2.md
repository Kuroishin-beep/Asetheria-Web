# Syndicate of the Justicar

Dedicated To: Themis
Organization: Titanic Organization
Subcategory: The Impartial
Type: Secret Society

The Syndicate of the Justicar moves in silence, balancing scales no court dares to weigh. Hidden from the world, its members deliver judgment beyond mortal laws, guided by the divine impartiality of Themis. Justice, to them, is not always seen—but it is always served.
