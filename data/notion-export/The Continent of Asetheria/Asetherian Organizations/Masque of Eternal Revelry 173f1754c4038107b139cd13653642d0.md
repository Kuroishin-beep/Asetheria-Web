# Masque of Eternal Revelry

Dedicated To: Bacchus
Organization: Invictian Organization
Subcategory: The Occult
Type: Secret Society

Operating in shadowed corners of the empire, the Masque of Eternal Revelry is a secret society devoted to ecstatic rites and divine celebration. Members wear elaborate masks and indulge in rituals that blur the lines between euphoria and mysticism. Few know their true purpose, but rumors speak of unending revels touched by occult forces.
