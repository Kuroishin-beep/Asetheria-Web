# About Asetheria

**Asetheria** is a homebrew D&D 5e world: a continent of three empires around an
inland sea, five years after the treaty that ended a fifteen-hundred-year war.

- **Imperium Invicta** in the west — Latin-flavoured, an empty Imperator's chair,
  an army that never went home.
- **Hellenoria** in the north — Greek-flavoured city-states under an emperor no
  one has seen in ten years.
- **Acheaoria** in the east — Persian-flavoured satrapies under a King of Kings,
  held together by qanats and fire-temples.

Between them stands **Deiperduem**, the neutral city where the peace was signed.

## This site

**Asetheria-Web** is the world's private codex and campaign manager — the place
the DM writes the world and the players read it.

- **One searchable codex:** 7,563 entries — cities, houses, gods, orders,
  champions, creatures, spells, quests, systems and lore — with fuzzy and
  semantic search and two-way `[[wiki links]]` (104,000 of them).
- **Secrets stay secret:** each player has their own login and per-player
  access; DM-only entries and DM notes are filtered out in the database before
  anything reaches a player.
- **Two books:** the Players' Guide for the table and the DM Codex for the DM.
- **At the table:** maps, a campaign calendar and timeline, a session planner,
  characters, dice and roll tables.
- **Nothing is lost:** archive instead of delete, full revision history on every
  edit made in the app.

Built with Next.js, TypeScript, Neon Postgres + pgvector and Drizzle, and
deployed on Netlify.
